# Reference notes: communicating about invoices (CC0-1.0)

These notes are a public-domain reference used only to check that generated prompt packs
are not near-duplicates of existing written material. They are deliberately written in a
different register from PromptFoundry products.

A reminder should state the invoice reference, the amount, the original due date and the
action required. Short sentences help. Avoid adjectives that imply blame. Offer a channel
for questions and, where relevant, a payment link. If the customer has raised a dispute,
acknowledge it before repeating the request, and separate the disputed items from the
undisputed balance. Escalate to a human when the customer threatens litigation, mentions
insolvency, or disputes more than one line item.

Final notices usually include a deadline and describe the next step without threatening
language. Many businesses include a phone number and hours. Keep a copy of every message
in the accounting system so the history is auditable.
